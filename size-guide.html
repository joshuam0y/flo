Chest width

Lay the shirt flat and measure straight across, 1 inch below the armpits. Double it for the full chest.

Body length

From the highest point of the shoulder, next to the collar, straight down to the bottom hem.

Sleeve

Tees and long sleeves: shoulder seam to cuff. Crewnecks: from the centre of the back collar, over the shoulder, down to the cuff.

Sweatpants

Waist: lay them flat and measure straight across the top, unstretched. Inseam: from the crotch seam down the inside leg to the cuff.
