The house

Maison de confort

Designer cuts. Everyday softness.

fló is a comfort house. We take the proportions, weights and finishing of the luxury labels we grew up wanting and put them into the pieces you actually wear every day: the hoodie, the sweatpant, the heavy tee.

Everything is made to order. Nothing is produced until someone wants it, so there's no overstock, no landfill, and no end-of-season sales to clear shelves.

Shipping

3–7 business days

Each order is made for you, then shipped with tracking. Free shipping on orders over $150; a flat $8 below that.

Returns

Made for you

Because every piece is made to order, we don't accept change-of-mind returns. If anything arrives damaged, misprinted or wrong, email us within 30 days and we'll replace it.

Sizing

Relaxed fit

Our tops run slightly oversized. Take your usual size for the intended look, or size down for a closer fit. Detailed measurements are listed on each product.

The list

First to know.

Drops are made in small runs. Join the list for early access, restocks and the occasional letter from the house.
